# Extracting Product Announcements from SEC 8-K Filings

Sebastian Esteban | November 4, 2026

GitHub: https://github.com/sebastiane64/LLMAssignment

## Objective and data selection

This project extracts named product announcements from SEC Form 8-K filings into five fields: company_name, stock_name, filing_time, new_product, and product_description. The Python notebook runs in Google Colab and queries the OpenAI API using gpt-5-mini. The sample selects up to twenty recent 8-K filings for each of AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, AMD, INTC, GOOGL, AMZN, META, TSLA, and QCOM, for 200 selected filings. This purposive sample emphasizes technology companies and is not representative of all public companies.

## Collection and preprocessing

The SEC company_tickers.json file maps ticker symbols to zero-padded CIK identifiers. The SEC submissions JSON endpoint provides company names, accession numbers, primary document names, and acceptance timestamps. This uses the documented submissions API instead of the assignment’s Atom-feed example. Filing time is the SEC acceptance timestamp, retaining its UTC designation when present; it is not a product launch date. BeautifulSoup parses the filing index and extracts readable text from the primary 8-K and linked EX-99 HTML/text press-release exhibits. Scripts, styles, and inline-XBRL header content are removed and whitespace is normalized. PDF exhibits are not processed. Sequential requests are paced at least 0.25 seconds apart and downloaded responses are cached. A contact email is supplied through the User-Agent.

## NER and LLM extraction

The model explicitly labels relevant company and product entities as ORGANIZATION and PRODUCT. This is prompted LLM-based named entity recognition, rather than a separately trained NER classifier. A Pydantic schema structures entities and product records. Each candidate must include a product name, a concise description, and a verbatim announcement quote. Instructions exclude routine mentions, financial results, and speculative development. Documents are split into overlapping 24,000-character chunks with 1,500-character overlap. All chunks are processed; text is not silently limited to its beginning. Document content is treated as evidence rather than instructions. Company, ticker, and timestamp fields are populated from SEC metadata rather than generated by the model.

## Validation and reproducibility

The pipeline verifies that the evidence quote occurs in the supplied text and contains the proposed product name, using case-insensitive whitespace normalization. Missing or unsupported candidates are rejected. Descriptions are limited to fewer than 180 characters; overly long text is shortened at a word boundary. A precision patch requires announcement language and merges simple company-prefix aliases. These checks reduce errors but do not establish that the launch verb refers to the correct product. Successful filing results are checkpointed, while errors are logged separately. The API key is entered through a hidden prompt and is not embedded in the submitted code.

## Observed results and evidence review

The expanded run selected and successfully processed 200 filings from ten companies. An initial Tesla ValidationError resolved on retry; the historical error remains in the log but no selected filing remained unsuccessful. The model produced 178 candidate rows across 34 filings, with 410 additional candidates rejected by extraction checks. Recorded successful-run usage was 1,041,962 input tokens and 89,176 output tokens. These counts exclude earlier test runs and may omit usage from failed requests. An assisted review of all 178 supplied candidate quotes excluded 33 rows, leaving 145 product records across 30 filings. The other 170 successful filings had no retained product records; this is not proof that they contained no announcements.

## Review decisions and limitations

Review removed same-filing aliases, announcements about third-party products, regional expansions of existing products, unnamed models, coalitions, prospective launches, and operational milestones without an explicit new product announcement. For example, “AMD Helion” duplicated “AMD Helion reference architecture”; Googlebook device announcements did not establish a new release of Intel’s processor; and OCI Compute E7 was Oracle’s product rather than AMD’s. Selected descriptions were revised to remove unsupported detail or promotional language. Named previews, new versions, and explicitly sampled products were retained when their quotes supported the announcement. Repeated products in different filings remain separate records because the unit is a product announcement within a filing. Review used the supplied evidence quotes, not an independent re-download of all SEC source pages. Therefore it does not establish precision, recall, complete coverage, or the absence of missed products. Borderline cases and cross-chunk context remain limitations.

## Deliverables and sources

products.csv contains only the five required columns. products_pipe.txt provides the pipe-separated alternative. evidence_reviewed.csv preserves quotes and source links; review_log.csv records every inclusion or exclusion; filing_audit.csv tracks successful filings; and the error log preserves the resolved initial validation error. The raw 200-filing run is preserved in raw_results.zip. review_results.py and review_decisions.csv reproduce the final review from that exact archive using a content-hash guard.
